# Rarewood catalog image art direction

The first catalog image pass uses original AI-generated editorial product illustrations, saved as 640 px WebP files with 240 px responsive variants in `public/images/wood/`.

These images are representative artwork, not photographs of supplier lots. They do not establish species identity, live inventory, dimensions, grade, origin, certification, or availability. Supplier lot photography should replace the artwork when a broker confirms a specific offer.

## Prompt set

- **Exotic lumber:** Premium studio catalog image of assorted exotic hardwood boards with varied natural grain and tones, clean milled edges, warm neutral stone and workshop backdrop, soft daylight, square crop. Illustrative category artwork; no single species or supplier lot implied.
- **Hardwood decking:** Dense hardwood decking boards with visible linear grain and a clean profiled edge, shown as material stock rather than an installed project; warm neutral studio, square close crop. Illustrative category artwork.
- **Hardwood flooring:** Carefully arranged flooring planks and sample boards with natural grain, milled edges and tongue-and-groove detail; material only, no completed room. Illustrative category artwork.
- **Slabs and rounds:** Wide live-edge hardwood slab with natural irregular edges and round sections showing end grain; neutral studio, square composition. Illustrative category artwork, not a specific slab.
- **Plywood and panels:** Stack of plywood and veneer panels with layered edges and subtle wood-grain faces; close three-quarter view. Illustrative category artwork, not a certification or lot claim.
- **Purpleheart Lumber:** Purpleheart lumber represented by planed boards and an end-grain block with deep violet heartwood and clean edges; warm neutral studio. Illustrative representation, not inventory photography.
- **Wenge Lumber:** Planed Wenge boards with dark brown heartwood and fine black grain lines, clean end grain, warm neutral stone studio; illustrative representation.
- **Zebrawood Lumber:** Planed Zebrawood boards with dark irregular streaks on golden-brown grain, natural pattern and clean milled edges; illustrative representation.
- **Sapele Lumber:** Planed Sapele boards with reddish-brown tone and interlocked ribbon grain, clean edges and soft daylight; illustrative representation.
- **African Mahogany Lumber:** Planed African Mahogany boards with reddish-brown color and flowing grain, stacked in a neutral stone studio; illustrative representation.
- **Honduran Mahogany Lumber:** Planed Honduran Mahogany boards with warm salmon-to-reddish brown tone and fine flowing grain; illustrative representation.
